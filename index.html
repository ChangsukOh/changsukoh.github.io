I am a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Department of Radiological Sciences at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), co-advised by Dr. Kyung Sung and Dr. Ricky Savjani. My research focuses on medical image analysis, generative AI, and machine learning for healthcare.

I received my B.S. and M.S. degrees in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering in 2019 and 2021, respectively, and my Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering in 2024, all from Seoul National University, where I was advised by Dr. H. Jin Kim.
