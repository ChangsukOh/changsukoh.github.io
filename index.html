I am a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Department of Radiological Sciences at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), co-advised by Prof. Kyung Sung and Prof. Ricky Savjani. I received my B.S. and M.S. degrees in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering in 2019 and 2021, respectively, and my Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering in 2024, all from Seoul National University, where I was advised by Prof. H. Jin Kim. My research focuses on medical image analysis and generative AI, with particular interests in brain MRI analysis, unsupervised anomaly detection, and AI-driven prediction of brain metastasis.
